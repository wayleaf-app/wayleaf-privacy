# Through The Dark privacy policy

Last updated: **25 November 2026**

Through The Dark is a single-player game for Android. This policy explains what the game stores and
what leaves your device.

The short version: **Through The Dark collects no data, and nothing leaves your device.** There is no
account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What the developer collects

**Nothing.** The game has no network access: it does not request Android's internet permission, so it
cannot send anything anywhere. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting
code, and it does not use the advertising ID.

## What is stored on your device

The game stores no personal information. Anything it keeps, such as game progress, stays in its own
private app storage, which no other app can read and which uninstalling the game removes.

The game reads your device's motion sensors while you play, to control the game. Those readings are
used on the device only and are never stored or sent anywhere.

## Permissions

Through The Dark requests no permissions that need your approval.

## Children

The game has no social features, no chat, no in-app purchases and no advertising. It collects
nothing, so it collects nothing from children.

## Changes to this policy

The date at the top says when this policy last changed. If a future version of the game ever collects
anything, this policy will say so before that version ships.

## Contact
